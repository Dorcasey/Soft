About Gent Official

Traditional Wear, Tailored to You

At Gent Official, we believe a man's traditional wear is his signature. We source premium traditional fabrics — Cashmere, 7stars, Lana, Calany, Osha Mila, Swiss and Voile — in a wide range of colours, and tailor them into neat, well-finished outfits based on each client's request.

From hand-picked cloths to meticulously finished cufflinks and trad caps, every piece is curated for the man who refuses to compromise on quality.

  • Traditional fabric sourcing — Cashmere, 7stars, Lana, Calany, Osha Mila, Swiss, Voile
  • Fabrics available in different colours
  • Tailoring of sourced fabrics to client request
  • Handcrafted cufflinks & trad caps
